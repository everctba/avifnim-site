Third-party licenses

AVIFnim uses third-party components. Each one keeps its own license, and nothing in the AVIFnim agreement limits your rights over those components.

FFmpeg — GPL

AVIFnim runs FFmpeg as a separate program (child process, via command line) and does not link FFmpeg libraries into the application. The distributed binary is GPL and the corresponding source is available below.

  • Windows: FFmpeg 7.1 (gyan.dev “essentials” build) — source at ffmpeg-7.1.tar.xz and build scripts at gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds.
  • Linux: FFmpeg n8.1 (BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds GPL) — source at github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds and FFmpeg n8.1.
  • macOS: FFmpeg 6.1.1 (eugeneware/ffmpeg-static, tag b6.1.1) — source at github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static and ffmpeg-6.1.1.tar.xz.

The exact build configuration is printed by ffmpeg -version (also available in the app under Help → CLI) and in each provider's build scripts.
