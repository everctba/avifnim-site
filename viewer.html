Why AVIFnim Viewer?

A viewer that solves what the others ignore.

Real animated AVIF

Frames in the right order, continuous loop and a native fallback when the system WebView can't decode AVIF. Works on Windows, Linux and macOS.

WebP, GIF and sequences

Besides animated AVIF and WebP, it plays GIF and numbered PNG/JPG sequences in the folder — at 30 or 60 fps with a pill Auto play to turn it on/off whenever you want.

Real folder browsing

Arrow keys ←/→, natural sorting (frame_2 before frame_10) and prefetch of neighbouring files so switching images doesn't stutter.

As big as your screen

Zoom, pan, Fit, 100%, fullscreen (F11) and checkerboard for transparency. Double-click toggles between Fit and 100%.

Light, dark and two languages

Whole interface in Portuguese (BR) or English, with light or dark theme.

Privacy by default

Your files never leave the computer. No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is AVIFnim Viewer free?

Yes. It's free, open source and licensed under MIT. There's no “premium” version of the viewer.

Which formats does it open?

AVIF (still and animated), WebP (still and animated), GIF, PPM/PNM (P1–P6) and numbered PNG/JPG sequences. It also opens individual PNG and JPG files.

Do I need to install it?

On Windows, use the NSIS installer. On Linux, the .deb. On macOS, the .dmg (drag to Applications).

Why does macOS warn that the developer can't be verified?

The app isn't signed/notarised by Apple yet. On first launch: right-click the app → Open → Open. Then it opens normally.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing is local. The app has no telemetry and doesn't send images to servers.

Does it play animated AVIF on Linux?

Yes. When the system WebKitGTK doesn't support AVIF, the app uses a native Rust decoder as fallback.

Can I use it commercially?

Yes. The MIT license allows personal and commercial use, keeping the license notice.

I found a bug. Where do I report it?

On GitHub: github.com/everctba/AVIFnim-Viewer/issues — or through the support page.
