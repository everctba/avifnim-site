Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated: 20 November 2026

The AVIFnim Viewer is a local, free and open source viewer (MIT). This policy explains what the app does — and what it not does — with your data.

Summary

AVIFnim Viewer does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal data. It has no accounts, telemetry, analytics, ads or tracking. Images and folders are processed locally on your device. Settings are stored locally. The app does not require internet access; external links open in your browser. Contact: mayerdesigner@gmail.com.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the AVIFnim Viewer (the free viewer). The AVIFnim (paid converter) is a separate product with its own EULA. When this policy mentions “the app”, it refers to the Viewer.

2. Data the app collects

No personal data is collected, stored, transmitted or sold. The app has no accounts, login, telemetry, analytics, ads, trackers or crash reports.

3. Files, images and sequences

The images, folder and sequences you open are processed locally on your device. No file is sent to our servers or third parties. Prefetching neighbouring files keeps bytes in local memory so browsing stays fast; nothing leaves the computer.

4. Settings and data on the device

Preferences such as theme, language, zoom, sequence FPS and prefetch size are saved in the app's data folder:

  • Windows: %APPDATA%\com.avifnim.viewer
  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/com.avifnim.viewer
  • Linux: ~/.config/com.avifnim.viewer

This data is not synced or sent to the cloud. You can delete it manually at any time.

5. Network and external links

The application doesn't make network calls to work. When you click links to the site, GitHub, PayPal or the Microsoft Store, the link opens in your default browser. From there, those sites' policies apply.

6. Microsoft Store

If you install the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect usage and diagnostic data under the Microsoft privacy policy. We have no access to personal data collected by the Store. The Viewer is free; there are no charges or purchase data for the app.

7. Site

The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record IP address and technical access data under its policy. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts; Google may record your IP on that request. We don't use analytics cookies, pixels or our own trackers.

8. Email and contact

If you send an email to mayerdesigner@gmail.com, your address and the message will be used only to reply and resolve the matter. We don't add your email to marketing lists.

9. Children

The app collects no personal data and is not specifically directed at children. It is suitable for all ages.

10. Your rights (LGPD and GDPR)

Since the app collects no personal data, there's nothing of yours to access, correct or delete in the app. If you have contacted us by email, you can request access, correction or deletion of the message at any time at the same address.

11. Security

Processing is local and the Viewer's code is open (MIT), which allows auditing. There are no servers of ours to leak your files. Still, keep the operating system and the app up to date.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date and version number.

13. Contact

Privacy questions: mayerdesigner@gmail.com. For help with the application, use the support page.
